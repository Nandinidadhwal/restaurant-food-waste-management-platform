Recurring Waste Sources and Improvement Observations

Department Primary Category Dominant Reason Observed Pattern Improvement Observation
Hot Kitchen Grains and Rice Overproduction Large rice and curry batches left unserved after 9:30 PM Scale evening batch sizes to reservation counts and cook smaller late top-ups.
Cold Pantry Fresh Produce Spoilage Wilted greens and soft tomatoes found in back bins Enforce first-in, first-out bin rotation and daily morning produce checks.
Bakery Bakery and Pastries Unsold Items Extra bread and pastry trays remaining at closing Match final bake run to historical closing demand rather than fixed quotas.
Hot Kitchen Dairy and Proteins Incorrect Preparation Over-seasoned or overcooked portions rejected at pass Standardize recipe cards and weigh key ingredients at prep stations.
