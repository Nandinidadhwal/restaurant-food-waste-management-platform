Restaurant Food Waste Management Platform

Recording food waste by category, reason, quantity, date, and department to identify recurring sources.

Problem Statement

Restaurants discard food due to overproduction, spoilage, incorrect preparation, and unsold items, but managers often lack visibility into where waste is occurring.

Objective

Develop a system that records food waste by category and identifies recurring waste sources.

Platform Modules and Tracked Fields

Feature Details Captured
Waste Records Date, department, food category, waste reason, quantity, and item notes
Food Categories and Reasons Categories paired with overproduction, spoilage, incorrect preparation, and unsold items
Trend Dashboard Distribution of waste across reasons and food categories
Improvement Observations Actionable notes to reduce repeat waste in each department
