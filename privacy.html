Privacy Policy

How operational and log data is handled within the platform.

1. Data Recorded

The platform tracks operational food waste entries including dates, departments, food categories, waste reasons, quantities, item names, and improvement observations. It does not require personal banking or sensitive consumer profiles to function.

2. Access and Use

Recorded entries and trend summaries are intended for internal restaurant operations, kitchen evaluation, and project review. Avoid entering confidential personal contact details inside observation fields.

3. Policy Updates

Updates to this privacy notice will be published directly on this page whenever data handling practices change.
